## 1. Rules

- This is research, not a sales call. Listen; don't pitch until the last 5 minutes.
- Ask about **last month**, not "usually". Real numbers beat opinions.
- Never ask for, or accept, real applicant documents or personal data. If someone starts screen-sharing an applicant record, ask them to stop.
- Ask permission before recording. Store notes in [Owner to complete: private location — not the public repo]. Use an interview ID (I-01 … I-10), not names, in any shared summary.
- Invoices and supplier contracts only if **voluntarily** shared, and only commercial figures.
- Don't promise prices, dates or features. The price is a **hypothesis**.

## 3. Interview guide

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for your time. I'm building an identity-checking service for recruitment and contractor onboarding, and I want to understand how you do it today — what works and what doesn't. There are no right answers. Is it OK if I take notes / record?"

### A. Context (5 min)
1. What do you place/onboard — sectors, roles, permanent vs temporary vs contract?
2. Who is involved in onboarding a new worker, from offer to first shift?
3. Where does the identity check sit in that? What tools do you use (ATS, CRM, payroll)?

### B. Volume (5 min) — get numbers
4. **How many identity checks did you do last month?** How does that vary across the year?
5. What share are UK passports, UK driving licences, Irish passports, other passports, other documents? (fill the document mix grid)
6. What share of applicants check on a phone vs laptop?

### C. Time and effort (5 min)
7. **How long does one check take your team, start to finish?** Including chasing.
8. How many checks need a second look or a manager? Last month, roughly?
9. Who does the checking, and what does an hour of their time cost? (rough band is fine)
10. **Operational reviewers only:** walk me through the last difficult check you did. What made it hard? What did you do?

### D. Drop-off and applicant experience (4 min)
11. **Of the people you ask to verify, how many don't finish?** How do you find out? What do you do?
12. What do applicants complain about? Any accessibility or "my phone doesn't work" problems?
13. What happens when someone can't do the online check?

### E. Fraud and risk (5 min)
14. **In the last 12 months, how many cases of identity fraud or impersonation did you find?** How were they found?
15. **What did they cost you** — money, client relationships, time, penalties? Any you can put a number on?
16. Do clients audit how you check identity? What do they ask for?
17. Do you also do right-to-work, DBS or AML checks? How are those done? (Note: Vitalos Verify does **not** provide those — record the need, don't imply we do.)

### F. Current supplier and procurement (5 min)
18. Do you use an ID verification supplier now? Which? What do you pay per check / per month? (only if they want to share)
19. What would make you switch? What would stop you?
20. **What does your procurement need before using a new supplier?** (security questionnaire, ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials, DPA, insurance, pen test report, data in UK, references)
21. Who signs off a new supplier, and how long does that usually take?

### G. Price (4 min) — show only now
"We're testing a price of about **£99 a month plus £2.50 per completed verification**. That's a hypothesis, not an offer."
22. How does that compare with what you pay or would expect to pay?
23. At what per-check price would this be **too expensive** to consider? At what price would it seem **too cheap to trust**?
24. Would you prefer per-check, a monthly bundle, or an annual commitment?
25. Would you take part in a **small, capped pilot** (e.g. up to [proposed] verifications over 2–3 months)? Would you pay for it? Who would be the operational owner?

### Close (2 min)
26. Is there anyone else I should talk to — especially someone who does the checks?
27. Can I follow up with a short summary and, later, a demo with synthetic data?

## 5. Scoring rubric (0–3 per line, max 21)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volume | < 20/month | 20–99 | 100–499 | 500+ |
| Pain (time) | < 5 min/check, no complaints | 5–10 min | 10–20 min | > 20 min or a dedicated person |
| Pain (drop-off / experience) | Not an issue | Mentioned | Measured and > 10% | Losing placements because of it |
| Fraud exposure | None found | Suspected | 1–2 confirmed | Repeated or costly |
| Fit with scope (identity, not RTW/DBS/AML only) | Needs only scheme checks we don't offer | Mostly scheme checks | Mix | Identity assurance is the main need |
| Price acceptance | "Too expensive" ≤ £1.50 | £1.50–£2.49 | £2.50–£3.50 | > £3.50 or values the workflow |
| Pilot readiness | No | Interested, no owner | Owner named | Owner named and willing to pay / sign a letter of intent |
