### 1. Use case
The Customer intends to use Vitalos Verify to help confirm the identity of **adult** [applicants / contractors] during onboarding for [roles/business unit].

The service checks identity documents and, where the Customer chooses, a live selfie against the document. **It is not a right-to-work, DBS, immigration, AML or qualification check.** The Customer remains responsible for its hiring and onboarding decisions.

### 4. Success measures (to be agreed in the pilot plan)
- Share of applicants who start and complete without help — target [e.g. ≥ 85%]
- Share of completed checks needing manual review — target [e.g. < 15%]
- Reviewer minutes per reviewed case — target [e.g. less than the Customer's current process: [current minutes]]
- Applicant drop-off compared with the Customer's current process
- Reviewer and applicant feedback
- Zero data incidents

These are targets to test, not promised results.

### 5. Data roles
The Customer would be the **controller** of applicant data. Vitalos would be its **processor**, using named sub-processors (including an identity-check supplier). Before any real applicant is invited, the parties will sign a data processing agreement and the Customer will complete its DPIA (Vitalos will provide its processor assessment). Default retention: evidence deleted 30 days after the final decision; backups expire within 35 days — for the Customer to confirm.
